**Fantasy Football Recap: Week Highlights**

In a thrilling week of fantasy football, "Shippin Up" sailed past "Game of Jones" with a decisive 174.88 to 132.44 victory. Garrett Wilson's standout performance of 26.7 points, significantly surpassing his 16.09 projection, was pivotal in Shippin Up's win. Meanwhile, "Game of Jones" fell short of their projected score, with key players like Justin Jefferson underperforming.

"Red Zone Rockets" narrowly edged out "Final Deztination" in a nail-biting 151.94 to 149.78 showdown. Brock Bowers was the hero for the Rockets, scoring 27.6 points against a 14.18 projection, while "Final Deztination" lamented benching Stefon Diggs, whose 7.3 points would have been a better choice than Brian Thomas Jr.'s 1.8.

"OC Gang" defeated "Hurts Donit" 170.64 to 154.78, with Drake London's explosive 28.4 points leading the charge. Despite Jaxon Smith-Njigba's impressive 35.36 points, "Hurts Donit" couldn't overcome the deficit. Notably, Matthew Golden's 21.0 points on the bench outshone Amon-Ra St. Brown's 11.9.

Despite a solid performance from Tee Higgins, "Allen & Co." couldn't close the gap, and their bench player RJ Harvey outperformed Travis Etienne Jr. in the starting lineup.

This week showcased the unpredictable nature of fantasy football, with bench decisions and projection misses playing crucial roles in the outcomes.
