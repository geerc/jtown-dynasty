**Fantasy Football Week Recap**

In a thrilling week of fantasy football, "Comeback Cardinals" delivered a standout performance, defeating "OC Gang" with a commanding score of 217.56 to 178.86. Caleb Williams led the charge for the Cardinals with a staggering 37.26 points, far exceeding his projected 16.39, while Derrick Henry contributed 35.3 points, doubling his projection. Despite a strong showing from Kenneth Walker III with 34.1 points for OC Gang, it wasn't enough to close the gap.

"Game of Jones" also emerged victorious with a decisive 177.0 to 135.2 win over "Red Zone Rockets." Justin Jefferson was a key player, scoring 31.2 points, well above his 17.08 projection. The Rockets struggled with underperformances from key players like George Kittle, who only managed 3.2 points against an 8.37 projection.

In a nail-biter, "Shippin Up To Austin" narrowly edged out "Final Deztination" with a score of 153.1 to 150.66. Despite the loss, Isaiah Likely was a standout for Final Deztination, scoring 27.8 points from the tight end position, far surpassing his 9.48 projection. On the bench, Stefon Diggs outperformed starting wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase with 15.5 points compared to Chase's 3.2.

Lastly, "Allen & Co." secured a win against "Hurts Donit" with a score of 139.46 to 131.12. Josh Allen was instrumental in the victory, delivering a remarkable 35.66 points, well above his expected 19.32. However, Hurts Donit saw a bench player, Jayden Daniels, score 17.66 points, outperforming starting quarterback Jalen Hurts, who scored 24.72 points but was expected to do more.

Overall, this week highlighted the unpredictability of fantasy football, with several players exceeding expectations and bench decisions proving pivotal.
